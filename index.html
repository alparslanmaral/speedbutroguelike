INFERNO SPEED

Escape Hell one card at a time. Outpace the Devil before the clock burns out.

How to Play

  • Play a card from your hand onto a center pile if its rank is one higher or one lower than the pile's top card.
  • Click a card (or press keys 1-5) to play it. Click a pile first to choose where it goes.
  • Your hand refills from your stock automatically. Empty your hand and stock before the timer runs out to win the ante.
  • If neither you nor the Devil can move, new cards are flipped onto the center piles.
  • Win chips, buy jokers in the shop, and build synergies. Every 3rd ante is a Boss Ante with special restrictions.
  • Run out of time and your progress burns. Survive all 10 antes to escape.

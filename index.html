Inferno Speed

Clear every card before the clock runs out. Ten antes stand between you and the exit.

How to Play

  • Play a card from your hand onto a center pile if its rank is one higher or one lower than the pile's top card.
  • Click a card, or press 1–5. Click a pile first if you want to choose where the card goes.
  • Your hand refills from your stock automatically. Empty your hand and your stock before the timer ends to clear the ante.
  • Stuck? Press Space or the Flip button to turn new cards from the reserves onto the piles.
  • Clear antes to earn chips, then buy jokers in the shop and build synergies. Every 3rd ante is a Boss Ante with special restrictions and less time.
  • Run out of time and the run is over. All chips and jokers are lost.
